Petrobras receives payment of new installment under the Diesel Economic Subvention Program

—

Rio de Janeiro, June 30, 2026 – Petróleo Brasileiro S.A. – Petrobras informs that it has received payment of a new installment under the Economic Subvention Program for diesel oil commercialization, in the amount of R$ 1.1 billion, corresponding to the period from April 7 to 19, 2026 (Provisional Measure No. 1,340).

 

The total amount accumulated by Petrobras to date amounts to approximately R$ 2 billion.
